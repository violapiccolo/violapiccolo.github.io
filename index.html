Hi, I'm Nika!

I started playing piano when I was five, and it has shaped my life ever since. For over 12 years I've immersed myself in music, and now I want to share that same joy with your children.

I love working with young students because I remember exactly what it felt like to sit at the piano for the first time. My goal is to make every lesson encouraging and rewarding, so your child can build a genuine, lasting love of music.

How It Works

Getting started is simple and pressure-free — here's what to expect from your child's first weeks at the piano.

STEP 1

Reach Out

Tell me a little about your child — their age, interests, and any experience. I'll help you choose the right starting point.

STEP 2

Meet & Play

We begin with a relaxed $20 first lesson. It's a fun, no-pressure way to see if we're a great fit before committing.

STEP 3

Grow Together

From there we settle into weekly lessons tailored to your child — building skill, confidence, and a real love of music.
